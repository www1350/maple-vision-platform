# Vision training
Use authorized assets and synthetic scenes. Model camera and parallax independently from terrain. Compare generic detection, fine-grained and two-stage models. Compare nano/small/medium. Baseline disables unrealistic perspective, rotation, vertical flip and strong lighting changes. Evaluate animation, occlusion, overlapping sprites, damage text, camera shifts and cross-background generalization. Split recordings by session; independent held-out test set. Report precision, recall, mAP, small-object and occlusion recall, latency and memory. Version datasets, models and ONNX exports.
